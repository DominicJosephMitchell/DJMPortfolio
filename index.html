Combining Technology, Leadership, and Problem Solving

Information technology brings together many of my strengths and interests. I enjoy designing, creating, building, troubleshooting, and managing technical solutions while also having the opportunity to lead projects and collaborate with others.

Networking, systems administration, programming, and infrastructure management provide an ideal environment for me to apply my technical knowledge, creativity, leadership skills, and problem-solving abilities.

In 2018, I enrolled at the Institute of Entrepreneurial & Leadership. After graduating, I continued expanding my education and technical knowledge through additional training at General Assembly and Rhode Coders.

These experiences have helped me develop a broader understanding of technology while strengthening my ability to learn independently and adapt to new tools, technologies, and methodologies.

As a leader, business owner, and project manager, I have developed a diverse set of skills that translate directly into the technology industry.

These include leadership, organization, communication, attention to detail, project management, troubleshooting, perseverance, and analytical problem solving.

I am an outgoing and personable professional who continuously strives to improve my skills and expand my knowledge.

Whether through formal education, self-directed learning, or on-the-job experience, I have consistently demonstrated a commitment to professional growth and lifelong learning.

I am confident that my combination of technical expertise, leadership experience, adaptability, and commitment to continuous improvement would allow me to make a meaningful contribution to an organization while continuing to grow as an IT professional.

Life Beyond Technology

Outside of Work

I believe your career should be an important part of your life—but it shouldn't be your entire life. There is a world beyond the office, the keyboard, and the next big project, and I make sure to leave plenty of room for it.

Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with my family and, of course, I can never stay too far away from technology. Programming, coding, networking and learning new things are hobbies that often find their way into my downtime.

For me, curiosity doesn't clock out at 5 PM!

Most importantly, I am a devoted father, grandfather, son, and uncle. My family is at the center of everything I do, and I value the time we spend together.

Over the years, I've also learned that family isn't defined solely by blood. Family is the people who support you, believe in you, challenge you to grow, and are there when you need someone to lean on.

To me, strong relationships, trust, loyalty, and a sense of community are what make life meaningful.

At the end of the day, technology may be my profession, but family, relationships, and continuous growth are what keep me grounded—and give me plenty of reasons to keep moving forward.
